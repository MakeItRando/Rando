## 2. Home rev 3 — what is strong

- **Truth about playback** is a real differentiator: "Now playing · Playlist · Late drive · 01/09", bounded queues, play-again-pauses, "End of source. Playback stops here." Most apps hide this.
- **Journey path** is the signature: nobody else shows "3 of 7 artists, next Moni Gray" as a visible route. Keep investing here.
- **State-aware CTAs** (Start / Pause / Resume + Open Journey) are correct and feel professional.
- **Anti-slop discipline** worked: no gradients/glow, plain labels, metadata as material. The look reads like a tool made by people, not a generator.
- Light mode, ⌘K, song menu, queue drawer, shortcuts: the basics a power user expects are there.

## 3. Home rev 3 — what is weak (honest)

1. **There is no phone design.** At 390 px the top bar, filter tabs and player bar overlap (render: logo + search icon + 4 tabs squeezed, transport icons collide). For a teen audience, phone is the main screen. This is the biggest gap and must come before more desktop features.
2. **Scrubber** (owner's complaint is right): ~200 full-height bars across the whole bar = visual noise at 30 px tall. Proposal: a 3 px line with a round thumb, grows to 6 px on hover/drag, time bubble on drag; the waveform appears only in Now Playing (where it has room) or as a faint 12 px strip on hover.
3. **Why it feels "boring":**
   - Every cover is the same kind of flat geometric sleeve in a muted palette; no faces, photos or texture, so nothing pulls the eye. Real art will fix much of this, but placeholders should be more varied (photo-like textures, type-led sleeves, illustrated, duotone portraits).
   - Home is mostly *about you* (stage, stats, on repeat) and only lightly *about new music* (Moods, new releases). There's no "something new I didn't expect" moment — exactly the "vibe to explore" the owner misses.
   - Everything is static until you act. Nothing changes day to day.
4. **Feature weight:** Blend, devices, monthly recap, share cards and notifications need accounts/backend (V2-ish). They are fine as designed screens, but V1 must not ship them as fakes; the spec should mark each as V1 / V1-local / V2.
5. **Inconsistency with our own rules:** UPPERCASE letter-spaced labels ("THIS WEEK", "UP NEXT", "PLAYLISTS") are banned in DESIGN_PROGRAM principles yet used everywhere. Pick one: I recommend sentence-case 13 px medium labels.
6. "This week" (5h 12m + bars) is large real estate for a daily Home. It's motivating for teens, but could collapse to one line + recap card most days.
7. Prototype starts "playing" on load — fine for a demo, but the real app must never autoplay (D-019).

## 4. Ideas (ranked) to make Home and the app feel alive and worth coming back to

**P0 — for Home rev 4**
1. **Dig** (re-do the lost idea, properly): a daily stack of 10 songs you've never heard. Each card plays the 15-second hook; Keep (→ "Dug" playlist + Library) or Skip. Swipe on phone, ←/→ on desktop. Finite (10/day, then "Done for today — 4 kept"), no streaks. This is the "explore vibe" and very teen-native.
2. **Phone layout first-class:** bottom tab bar (Home, Journeys, Dig, Library), mini player above it with a 2 px progress line, full-screen Now Playing with swipe-down, Journey path as a horizontal scroller.
3. **Clean scrubber** as above.
4. **Hook previews everywhere:** long-press / hover a cover = 10 s hook with a tiny ring timer. Needs a `hookStart` per track in the data model.

**P1 — signature depth**
5. **Journey stamps:** finishing an artist "stamps" their sleeve into a Journey passport (per genre). Collectible, shareable, honest (based on real listening), no streak pressure.
6. **Living sleeves:** subtle audio-reactive motion on the playing cover (uses the analyser we already have), removed under Reduced Motion. Later: artist-supplied motion art.
7. **Daily drop row:** one editorial "Today" row that is different every day (new releases, a scene, a producer). Makes Home change.
8. **Share → listen loop:** share card links to a 30 s preview page with "Open in Rondo" — the main growth loop for teens (stories), with no tracking tricks.

**P2 — later / V2**
9. Credits graph (producer/writer hops), Blend and shared playlists (accounts), device handoff, Rondo Plus (payments are V2 only; value must be clear first: e.g. hi-fi, offline, unlimited Dig, early Journeys).

## 5. Competition (how Rondo wins)

Rondo will not beat big apps on catalog size. It wins on: (a) **guided depth** — Journeys and artist worlds; (b) **truth** — you always know what plays next and why; (c) **fast discovery that ends** — Dig instead of endless autoplay; (d) **craft** — a calm, precise, non-AI look teens can screenshot proudly. Every new feature should strengthen one of these four.
