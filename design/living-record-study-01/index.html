Scene rationale

Earn the first play.

One familiar artist is enough to begin. Account creation and preference tuning can happen after Rondo proves its value.

  • One decision, one primary action
  • Clear skip path without guilt
  • No abstract “taste calibration” language

A point of view—not an infinite feed.

A daily editorial lead gives Rondo a voice. Smaller shelves explain how far each recommendation moves from familiar territory.

  • Finite, calm recommendations
  • Visible recommendation rationale
  • Artwork carries color and emotion

A route through music.

Journeys are sequenced stories with progression and editorial context, visually distinct from playlists and search results.

  • Chapters instead of a song table
  • Future songs retain some surprise
  • No manipulative streaks or urgency

Make following feel meaningful.

Artist pages lead with identity, a human editorial entry point, and releases. Metrics stay secondary.

  • Functional follow ring
  • Clear “start here” recommendation
  • Artwork-first release browsing

The interface becomes quiet.

Explore’s warm surface falls away. The cover, one lyric, and the physical act of playback become the room.

  • Localized album-color reflection
  • Large, reachable transport controls
  • Reduced-motion crossfade equivalent

Design system

Recognizable through behavior.

The identity is not a collection of gradients. It is a consistent set of surfaces, states, geometry, and motion rules.
