# Your month — spec (Home E rev 19, D-087 proposal, 2026-10-10)

Owner (N9): "your september feature is already there… add things and make it better." Rule: any time, no streaks, no goals, nothing invented (D-019, D-050).

**Where:** Home → This week card → **Your September** ("Ready · or see October so far"), and the notification "Your September recap is ready". Library will also list it (Library design).

**Shape:** story cards (kept from the old recap), but you can *use* them, not just watch them.

## Months
- Tabs at the top: **September** (finished) · **October so far** (current month, says "so far" everywhere, future days shown as empty dashed squares). Older months come with Library.
- Every number on every card comes from one set of month numbers (minutes per day, plays per artist/song, plays per hour), so they always agree. (The old card said "Saturday the 20th" — the 20th was a Sunday.)

## Cards
1. **Overview** — calendar with weekday letters (month starts on the right weekday). Total time, song count, busiest day. **Tap a day** → that day's time, song count and top 3 songs; tap a song to play it. Quiet days say "Nothing played. A quiet day."
2. **Top artists** — top 5 with plays and a bar; tap one to open their artist page.
3. **Song of the month** — big cover with play, then #2–#5; each plays in place (the recap stays open).
4. **When you listen** — 24-hour clock, bars by hour; the busiest 3-hour stretch is highlighted and named ("Mostly between 9 pm and 12 am").
5. **Found** — new artists (+ how many through a Journey, Journeys finished); tap a face to open the artist.
6. **Moments you saved** — only when you saved moments that month (so October has it, September doesn't). Tap to play the moment.
7. **Your sound** — pace, when you listen most, top genre. **Play your September** (your most played songs, most played first), **Save as playlist** ("September 2026", once — a second tap says it's already there), **Save card**.

## Story controls
- Cards move on their own every 6.5 s **until you touch something** (tap a day, play a song) — then they stay put. Pause button next to ×; Space pauses; ← / → or tap the left/right edge of the card to move; the bars at the top jump to a card; hold to pause. Reduced motion: no auto-advance.
- Esc / × closes. Phone: full screen.

## Not added (on purpose)
- No streaks, "minutes goals", percentile bragging ("top 1% of listeners"), or AI-written summaries.
- No comparison with friends — profile & social phase.
