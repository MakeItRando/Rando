# Design reset — original Rondo is the foundation

## Confirmed owner verdict

The owner rejected the Living Record study as AI-looking, low quality, and worse than the original app. [PR #9](https://github.com/MakeItRando/Rando/pull/9) is closed, unmerged, at `dbf4389792ae993a2e5435798d95685f35c24468`. Its body and owner-context screenshot 05 record the rejection. No production application code was changed by that study.

**Do not merge, revive, blend, or quietly rebrand this rejected direction.** Preserve its branch only as negative evidence. The cream/serif editorial reset, oversized slogans, forced Living Record terminology, two-worlds concept, concept-board styling and generated mockups are not approved product requirements. Existing functional circular branding may stay; rejection of the concept does not require removing the original mark.

## Correct next design method

Work from the original candidate's dark musical atmosphere, artwork, navigation, artist pages and Song Room. Improve a specific observed weakness at real desktop/mobile sizes. Show before/after evidence in the existing product language. Reject change that is only different, not demonstrably clearer, more useful or better crafted. No wholesale redesign or generated concept-board round without a new explicit owner request.

The owner also criticized the existing onboarding's low-effort copy, color treatment and template-like craft. That criticism is not acceptance of the proposed replacement. Onboarding remains unresolved; refine the real flow, not an unrelated visual identity.

## Orientation recommendations — not approved scope

1. Reduce first-listen friction; consider letting a visitor play before email/genre/artist setup. This changes the current required-setup contract and needs an owner answer.
2. Show a quiet, understandable playback source and separate resumable intentions; fix continuity before adding a switcher.
3. Keep surfaces neutral enough for controls/metadata to remain readable. Use song color as controlled emphasis, not a page-wide saturation wash.
4. Prioritize art, track identity and action hierarchy over slogan size or decorative framing. Review small secondary text, focus and contrast at actual size.
5. Use familiar terms rather than internal strategy language such as doors, seeds and chapters where it makes the action less clear.
6. Keep delight causal: a response to play, queue edits, saved moments, or opening Song Room; Reduced Motion receives an equally complete experience.

For each approved refinement document surface/state, current problem, smallest change, acceptance criteria, screenshots, responsive/accessibility impact and rollback. See [AUDIT_2026-10-04.md](AUDIT_2026-10-04.md), [PRODUCT_MAP.md](PRODUCT_MAP.md) and [QUALITY_GATES.md](QUALITY_GATES.md).
