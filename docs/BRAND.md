# Rondo brand foundation

## Core

- **Name:** Rondo
- **Pronunciation:** RON-doh
- **Promise:** Find your next repeat.
- **Idea:** Music returns, changes, and leads somewhere new.

## Mark

The mark is a broken circular track with a vermilion playhead. It must remain recognizable at 16px, work in one color, and never be paired with a generic music-note symbol.

## Core palette

| Token | Hex | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ink | `#0B0B0C` | Navigation, player, primary type |
| Warm ivory | `#F3F0E9` | Main light canvas |
| Paper | `#FAF8F3` | Raised light surfaces |
| Signal red | `#FF4B2E` | Identity, playhead, and default action |
| Graphite | `#6D6962` | Secondary type |

## Listening signals

Night appearance uses near-black genre bases. The active genre can tint listening surfaces and interaction signals without replacing the core identity:

- Hip-Hop — ember `#FF6846` / ultraviolet `#8C63FF`
- R&B — rose `#FF79BD` / violet `#8B7BFF`
- Electronic — cyan `#47E0CF` / cobalt `#5F7CFF`
- Jazz — amber `#F4B85F` / burgundy `#C45F7D`

These colors are contextual, never new logos. Album artwork still leads. Every state remains legible without color.

## Voice

Short, assured, and specific. Avoid hype, filler, copied product language, and machine-like recommendation copy.

- Prefer: “Find your next repeat.”
- Prefer: “Continue to the next artist?”
- Prefer: “Up next in this artist chapter.”
- Avoid: “AI-powered personalized genre experience.”
- Avoid: claims that simulated motion is real audio analysis.

## Design rules

1. Album artwork leads; interface chrome stays neutral.
2. Use lines, alignment, scale, and whitespace before cards or shadows.
3. Reserve rounded pills for filters and compact status labels.
4. Motion follows playback, loading, or navigation state.
5. Night is the default; Light is equally complete and persistent.
6. Metadata distinguishes primary artists, featured artists, genres, styles, writers, and producers.
7. Genre signals adapt the atmosphere while the Rondo mark and wordmark remain constant.
