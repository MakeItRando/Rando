## Verdict

**Do not request the owner to run the acceptance test yet.** The attached desktop Lyrics image visibly renders `undefined` in Song Room's tiny transport metadata (`142 BPM · F MINOR · undefined · RONDO ORIGINAL`). This is a genuine user-facing product defect not caught by current smoke/visual assertions. Source at [candidate `src/app.js`, `renderFullPlayer`](https://github.com/MakeItRando/Rando/blob/cd25bbda8b4e92671c9a61fd97352b9eaac6fc1d/src/app.js) assembles `audioMeta(track) · ${palette.signal} · playbackSource(track)`, while [getArtworkPalette](https://github.com/MakeItRando/Rando/blob/cd25bbda8b4e92671c9a61fd97352b9eaac6fc1d/src/ui/songRoom.js) returns `id`, `accent`, `rgb`, `accentSoft`, `base`, `surface`, `elevated`, **not `signal`**. Remove the unsupported segment and let the independent, truthful waveform state own any `Live signal`/`Playback motion` label. Add a regression asserting rendered metadata never contains `undefined`/`null`, in both Song Room and compact/mobile states. Do not paper this over with invented metadata.

## UX findings from the supplied images

1. **320px Discover:** the search input's hint is visibly truncated (`Search songs, artists, or album...`), and a stacked ~three-line hero plus CTA consumes nearly the entire content window. At 320×700 only the heading `Hits today` reaches the bottom navigation, with no playable song visible above it. The mobile 390px image is more useful but still spends most of the first screen on intro/CTA/search before music. Shorten the compact hero or rearrange its vertical rhythm so a playable selection appears sooner, without making touch targets smaller; use shorter search placeholder/copy and an accessible label rather than relying on clipped placeholder text. Verify 320px, 390px, large text and zoom.
2. **Artist mobile:** the R&B artist capture has useful art, hierarchy and a clear `Play artist` action, but a transient `R&B journey loaded` toast overlaps the track list in the capture. Confirm it disappears promptly, never blocks interaction/focus, and does not persist in screenshots meant to judge the stable page. The image alone cannot prove a permanent overlap.
3. **Artwork differentiation:** covers in Light Discover and Hip-Hop songs are coherently art-directed but share very similar concentric/edition motifs. Avoid filler-like repetition when broadening the catalog; make release-specific identity and authorized artwork a curation standard, not merely palette swaps. This is a craft note, not a confirmed blocking bug in the fictional prototype.
4. **Desktop scale and legibility:** the genre title is large and the desktop Song Room right-side panel uses small muted labels; inspect at actual size, keyboard focus and zoom before signing off. The desktop Song Room composition is otherwise coherent, with art central and transport separated. Avoid promising accessibility based on pixels alone.
5. **What works:** the Journeys hero has clear primary/secondary actions and progress, mobile genre composition remains readable with distinct persistent player/navigation rows, Light Discover is consistent, and Reduced Motion Song Room clearly labels static/reduced motion without an apparent page overflow. These are visual impressions from provided images, not runtime or keyboard proof.
